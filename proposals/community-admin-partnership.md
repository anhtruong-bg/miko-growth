## 1. Background

Miko Challenge aims for about **100 sign-ups** so we can pick the top teams. Dr. Michael's fanpage does not reach enough people on its own, so we need to go through the admins of large tech and startup Facebook groups.

These admins may not need Miko themselves. So instead of offering a ready-made benefits package, the program **lets admins use Miko for real first**, then agrees a partnership tier based on how satisfied they are. An admin who has seen the value will recommend Miko to members naturally, without it feeling like an ad.

## 2. How it works

### Step 1: Invite to try (minimum benefit for every admin)

- The admin gets access to **all 33 Miko tasks**, free, with no strings attached.
- To fit the challenge schedule, the admin **only needs to go through the first 7 tasks** (from idea to prototype) to decide.
- A **30-minute onboarding session** with Tiến or Dr. Michael, so the admin sees the value quickly instead of figuring it out alone.

### Step 2: Collect feedback

After the trial, ask the admin the [questions in section 5](#5-post-trial-questions).

### Step 3: Agree a partnership tier

| | **Core partner** | **Basic partner** |
|---|---|---|
| **Criteria** | Recommendation score of 8/10 or higher, and commits to take part (posting, pinning posts, judging or speaking) | Admin finds Miko fine and agrees to share it, but commits to less |
| **Benefit for group members** | Free access up to **task 20**, limited seats (proposed 2–5 teams per group) | Free access to **7 tasks**, unlimited seats, plus a group-only perk (see section 3) |
| **Benefit for the admin** | Keeps the full 33-task account. **Judge or speaker** at the final event. Group name and logo on the website, posters and video. | Keeps the full 33-task account. Group name in the list of supporting communities. |
| **Support from Miko** | Ready-made content kit, group-specific sign-up link, AMA or livestream with Dr. Michael in the group, results report after the challenge | Ready-made content kit, group-specific sign-up link, results report after the challenge |

## 3. Making the "free 7 tasks" tier worth something

Under the challenge plan, **everyone can do the first 7 tasks for free** once it is announced in early December. Without something extra, members of a basic partner group will not feel they get anything special. Pick one or more of these perks:

- **Priority in selection:** teams from partner groups get priority when choosing who gets access up to task 20.
- **More time:** a longer deadline for the 7 tasks, or access that continues after the challenge ends.
- **Group identity:** a sign-up link carrying the group's name, plus a leaderboard for that community.

> **To decide:** which perk the basic tier gets.

## 5. Post-trial questions

1. On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to recommend Miko to your group's members?
2. What part of Miko did you find most useful for your members?
3. What still makes you hesitate?
4. How would you like to take part?
   - [ ] Post about the challenge (about how many posts?)
   - [ ] Pin the post in the group
   - [ ] Judge or speak at the final event
   - [ ] Host an AMA or livestream with Dr. Michael in the group
5. What do your members usually struggle with when starting a business?

## 6. How to approach admins

### 6.1. With a warm contact

The person who knows the admin reaches out directly, by message or call, in their usual tone. No template needed. Just make sure the conversation covers:
- An invitation to **try all 33 tasks, free, no strings attached**, with a quick 30-minute walkthrough.
- If the admin likes it, then we discuss a partnership for the group (see section 2).
- A feedback deadline of **Nov 28**, so we are ready for the early-December announcement.

If the connection is only indirect, ask the mutual contact to introduce you first, then message.

### 6.2. Without a warm contact

The first message must be **friendly, like one community member talking to another**. Do not open with a sales pitch or a partnership offer. The message must cover three points:
1. **Miko is built for the group's own members.** Michael and the team built Miko with a lot of care, based on Michael's own startup experience and the gaps he sees today's builders need to close to succeed.
2. **The potential of AI and Vietnamese talent.** AI is opening a huge opportunity, and Vietnamese people are fully capable of building world-class products.
3. **The pain point in the job market.** Many tech workers fear being replaced, are strong technically but lack business thinking, and build many products that nobody pays for yet.

**Before sending:**
- Replace everything in square brackets. **[something you noticed in the group]** should be a real detail (a post, a meetup, one of the admin's own products), so the admin can tell this is not a mass message.
- Only send details about Miko Challenge and the walkthrough once the admin replies.

## 8. What to avoid

- **Offering cash or commission upfront.** Posts would look like ads. Save commission for the affiliate program later (20% off for buyers, 10% commission for referrers).
- **"Course" messaging.** Stress **"build for real, ship a real product"** and the chance to join the venture builder.
- **Overpromising.** Resources are focused on the challenge, so cap the number of task-20 seats and AMA sessions clearly from the start.
